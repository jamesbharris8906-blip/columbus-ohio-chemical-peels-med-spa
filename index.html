A chemical peel is a liquid put on the skin that helps old, dull cells lift off. New, fresher skin shows underneath. It sounds strong, but a light peel is gentle, and it is done in a calm room by a trained provider. What a Peel Feels Like

A peel visit starts with a clean face. The provider puts the peel liquid on in layers, and you may feel a tingle, warmth, or a light sting for a short time. That feeling fades fast. The provider watches your skin the whole time, and they will tell you what is normal and what is not.

After a light peel, skin may look a bit pink, then bright. After a medium or deep peel, skin may flake or peel for a few days, like a sunburn peeling off. Do not pick at the flakes. Let them fall off on their own. Use gentle wash, moisturizer, and sunscreen, and stay out of strong sun. A Columbus Ohio Chemical Peels Med Spa visit is a start, and home care in the first week makes a big difference.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which peel should I try first?

Most people start with Illuminize, the lightest peel. It gives a bright look with little downtime, and it lets you see how your skin reacts. Your provider can then talk about a stronger peel next time, if you want one.

Will my skin peel a lot?

It depends on the peel. A light peel may cause almost no visible peeling. A medium or deep peel may cause flaking for a few days. Do not pick at flakes, and follow the home care notes your provider gives you.

Does a peel hurt?

Most people feel a tingle or warmth for a short time. It is not the same for everyone, and your provider will watch your skin and check in with you. Tell them if the feeling is too strong.

How long until I see a change?

A light peel can give a bright look in a day or two. A medium or deep peel may take a week or more, as skin flakes and fresh skin shows. Take a photo before, and another one a week later, to see the change.

Can I wear makeup after a peel?

Your provider will tell you when it is safe, often after a day or two for a light peel, and longer for a deeper one. Use a light layer at first, and skip harsh products until your skin feels calm.

How often can I get a peel?

Light peels can be done every few weeks, while deeper peels need more time between visits. Your provider will build a plan based on your skin, your goals, and how your skin heals.
